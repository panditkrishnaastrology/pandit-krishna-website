About

Astrologer and professor

Pandit Krishna is an astrologer and a professor. For more than 19 years he has guided people through life’s important questions using the classical systems of Jyotish: Vedic astrology, Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, Prasna Jyotish and Vedic numerology. He learned astrology from his guru, late Shri Narayan Guruji, and carries that tradition forward in every reading and every class.

His particular strength is Nadi astrology. Through Bhrigu Nandi Nadi he reads the story of a chart and times key events such as career changes, marriage, property, and periods of growth or caution. For urgent matters he uses Kasyapa Hora, the Prasna section of Nadi astrology, which answers a single question from the moment it is asked, without needing a birth chart.

As a professor, he holds a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. That training shapes how he works: every prediction is reasoned step by step from the chart, and he explains the “why” behind it, not just the answer.

He consults in Odia, Hindi and English, in person in Bhubaneswar and online worldwide, and teaches structured courses for students who want to learn the classical methods properly, from the basics to advanced Nadi techniques.

Our emblem

The Shatkona in the lotus

Our emblem brings together three sacred forms of the Hindu tradition. Each one says something about how we practise Jyotish.

Shatkona

Two interlaced triangles: Shiva, consciousness and fire, rising upward; Shakti, energy and water, flowing downward. Their union is all creation. The six points recall the six Vedangas, of which Jyotisha is the eye.

Bindu

The point at the centre is the source from which creation begins and to which it returns. A point within a circle is also the ancient sign of Surya, the soul, from which every chart is read.

Sixteen-petalled lotus

Eight outer petals face the eight directions guarded by the Ashta Dikpalas. With the eight inner petals they make sixteen, the kalas of the Moon, ruler of the mind. Like the lotus, the soul can rise pure above its karma.

Gold and the dark sky

Gold is the colour of Surya and of Guru, the planet of wisdom and teaching. The deep centre is Akasha, the night sky of the grahas, and recalls the dark form of Krishna.

To read the light of the grahas with wisdom, and help each seeker rise like the lotus.

Know your auspicious time

Choose the purpose, and add your lagna, rashi and birth nakshatra if you know them for times suited to you. See the auspicious times for the next 7 days.

Purpose
How we choose the times
  • Panchanga shuddhi: the tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and weekday must suit the purpose. Rikta tithis (Chaturthi, Navami, Chaturdashi), Amavasya, Vishti (Bhadra) karana and the Vyatipata and Vaidhriti yogas are avoided.
  • Inauspicious periods are avoided: Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Durmuhurta, Varjyam and Gandanta.
  • Special yogas are shown with the times they cover, for your information: Sarvartha Siddhi, Amrita Siddhi, Ravi, Guru Pushya and Ravi Pushya (auspicious); Dwipushkar and Tripushkar (the result of an act repeats); Mrityu, Utpata, Yamaghanta, Dagdha and Visha (inauspicious). They do not change the times offered.
  • Tara bala and Chandra bala are checked from your birth nakshatra and rashi. Vipat, Pratyak and Naidhana taras are avoided.
  • Lagna shuddhi: the rising lagna should suit the purpose, and the Moon should not be in the 6th or 8th from it. Jupiter or Venus in a kendra strengthens the time; malefics in the 1st or 8th weaken it. Abhijit muhurta adds strength.
  • Marriage: only the marriage nakshatras; the 7th and 8th houses from the lagna must be empty; no marriage during Chaturmas, Kharmas, or when Jupiter or Venus is combust.
  • Travel: Disha shool and Panchaka are considered. Purchase and sale follow the kraya and vikraya nakshatras.

Based on the classical Muhurta Chintamani. Pandit Krishna also applies his own principles from his practice, which are not listed here.
