Pandit Krishna is an astrologer and a professor. For more than 19 years he has guided people through life’s important questions using the classical systems of Jyotish: Vedic astrology, Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, Prasna Jyotish and Vedic numerology. He learned astrology from his guru, late Shri Narayan Guruji, and carries that tradition forward in every reading and every class.

His particular strength is Nadi astrology. Through Bhrigu Nandi Nadi he reads the story of a chart and times key events such as career changes, marriage, property, and periods of growth or caution. For urgent matters he uses Kasyapa Hora, the Prasna section of Nadi astrology, which answers a single question from the moment it is asked, without needing a birth chart.

As a professor, he holds a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. That training shapes how he works: every prediction is reasoned step by step from the chart, and he explains the “why” behind it, not just the answer.

He consults in Odia, Hindi and English, in person in Bhubaneswar and online worldwide, and teaches structured courses for students who want to learn the classical methods properly, from the basics to advanced Nadi techniques.
