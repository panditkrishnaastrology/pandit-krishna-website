What it does

Choose a purpose: General, Marriage, Travel, Meeting, Job, Dating, Purchase, Sale. The finder checks the Panchang for each day and lists the good times with a rating of Good, Very good or Excellent, and the reasons for each. It runs on your own device, so your details are never sent anywhere.

How the times are chosen

  • Panchanga shuddhi: the tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and weekday must suit the purpose. Rikta tithis (Chaturthi, Navami, Chaturdashi), Amavasya, Vishti (Bhadra) karana and the Vyatipata and Vaidhriti yogas are avoided.
  • Inauspicious periods are avoided: Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal, Durmuhurta, Varjyam and Gandanta.
  • Special yogas are shown with the times they cover, for your information: Sarvartha Siddhi, Amrita Siddhi, Ravi, Guru Pushya and Ravi Pushya (auspicious); Dwipushkar and Tripushkar (the result of an act repeats); Mrityu, Utpata, Yamaghanta, Dagdha and Visha (inauspicious). They do not change the times offered.
  • Tara bala and Chandra bala are checked from your birth nakshatra and rashi. Vipat, Pratyak and Naidhana taras are avoided.
  • Lagna shuddhi: the rising lagna should suit the purpose, and the Moon should not be in the 6th or 8th from it. Jupiter or Venus in a kendra strengthens the time; malefics in the 1st or 8th weaken it. Abhijit muhurta adds strength.
  • Marriage: only the marriage nakshatras; the 7th and 8th houses from the lagna must be empty; no marriage during Chaturmas, Kharmas, or when Jupiter or Venus is combust.
  • Travel: Disha shool and Panchaka are considered. Purchase and sale follow the kraya and vikraya nakshatras.

Based on the classical Muhurta Chintamani. Pandit Krishna also applies his own principles from his practice, which are not listed here.
