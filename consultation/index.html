How a consultation works

Send a WhatsApp message or call with the service you want and a preferred day and time. For a birth-chart reading, share your date, exact time and place of birth; Prasna Jyotish needs only your question. Payment is by UPI or bank transfer before the session. After the reading, a written report is sent as a PDF, free of charge.

Readings depend on the birth details you give, so please check them before your session. See the Refund Policy and Disclaimer.

Questions

How do I book a consultation?

Send a WhatsApp message or call +91 96680 24876 with the service you want and a preferred day and time. You will get a reply confirming the slot and fee.

What details do I need for a reading?

Your date of birth, exact time of birth and place of birth. Prasna Jyotish needs no birth details, only your question.

Can I consult from another city or country?

Yes. Consultations are online for clients anywhere in the world, and in person in Bhubaneswar by appointment. How to join is shared on WhatsApp when your slot is confirmed.

How do I pay?

By UPI or bank transfer before your session. Payment details are sent on WhatsApp from +91 96680 24876 when your slot is confirmed.

Will I get a written report?

Yes. A written report of your reading is sent as a PDF, free of charge.

Which languages do you consult in?

Odia, Hindi and English.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Cancel 24 hours or more before your slot for a full refund or a free change of date. With less notice there is no refund, but you may reschedule once within 30 days. The Refund Policy has the full terms.
