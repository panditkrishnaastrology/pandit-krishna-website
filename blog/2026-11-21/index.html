Prabodhini Ekadashi: the Moon meets Saturn

Saturday, 21 November 2026 · Pandit Krishna, Bhubaneswar

Today is Prabodhini (Devutthana) Ekadashi, by the Bhubaneswar Panchang. Tradition says Lord Vishnu wakes today from His four-month rest (Chaturmas), and auspicious work such as marriages begins again from this time. Many families also perform Tulasi Vivah around these days.

The Moon and Saturn sit together today in Meena (Pisces), both in Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra, which is ruled by Saturn. In Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, the Moon stands for the mind and the mother, and Saturn for work and karma. When the two join, BNN reads a serious, patient mind that takes duty to heart. This is a good day for steady, quiet work, study and prayer, rather than haste.

Student exercise: in your own chart, see whether the Moon and Saturn are in the same sign, or in signs 1, 5 and 9 from each other.

Simple remedy: offer Tulasi leaves to Lord Vishnu and chant "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" 108 times.

Note: Rahu Kaal today is 8:45 AM to 10:09 AM.
