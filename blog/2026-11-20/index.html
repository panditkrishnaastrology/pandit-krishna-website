Jupiter and Mars together in Leo

Friday, 20 November 2026 · Pandit Krishna, Bhubaneswar

Today Jupiter and Mars sit together in Simha (Leo), both in Magha nakshatra, less than 2° apart. In Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, Jupiter stands for the native (jeeva) in a man's chart, and Mars for courage, energy, brothers and land. When the two join, BNN reads a person who acts boldly but keeps to principle. It can also point to work linked to land and property, or a close bond with a brother.

Magha is ruled by Ketu, and its devata is the Pitris (ancestors). So this pair also asks us to honour family tradition.

Student exercise: in your own chart, look for Jupiter and Mars in the same sign, or in signs 1, 5 and 9 from each other. Note which planet is stronger.

Festival: Prabodhini (Devutthana) Ekadashi falls tomorrow, 21 November, by the Bhubaneswar Panchang.

Simple remedy: on Thursdays, offer yellow flowers or chana dal at a temple and chant "Om Gurave Namah" 108 times.

Note: Bhadra (Vishti karana) runs from 7:00 PM tonight. Traditionally, new work is not started during Bhadra.
