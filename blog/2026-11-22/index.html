Ravi Pradosh: Lord Shiva's evening on Trayodashi

Sunday, 22 November 2026 · Pandit Krishna, Bhubaneswar

Today is Shukla Trayodashi of Kartika month, by the Bhubaneswar Panchang. The Trayodashi tithi runs through the evening, so today is Pradosh Vrat. When Pradosh falls on a Sunday, it is called Ravi Pradosh.

Pradosh means the sacred time around sunset. On this tithi, devotees worship Lord Shiva and Mother Parvati in the evening. Tradition says Shiva is pleased by simple devotion at this hour: water, milk and bilva leaves offered with a calm mind. On Trayodashi tithi, worship of Lord Nandi is also beneficial.

Sunday is ruled by the Sun, the karaka of father, authority and self-respect. So Ravi Pradosh is a good day to pray for good health of the father, clarity in duty and steady confidence.

The Moon is in Mesha, in Ashwini nakshatra, ruled by Ketu. Ashwini supports a fresh, quick start in prayer and self-discipline. Vyatipata yoga lasts till 11:29 PM, which is traditionally avoided for new ventures, so keep the day for worship and routine work.

Remedy: After sunset, offer water and bilva leaves to a Shiva Linga and chant "Om Namah Shivaya" 108 times.

Rahu Kaal today is 3:42 PM to 5:05 PM. Begin the Pradosh puja after sunset (5:05 PM, Bhubaneswar).
