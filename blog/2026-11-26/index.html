Mrigashira Nakshatra: the gentle star of search

Thursday, 26 November 2026 · Pandit Krishna, Bhubaneswar

Today is Guruvara, Krishna Dwitiya. The Moon moves through Mrigashira nakshatra until 5:47 PM, and in the early morning it is exalted in Vrishabha.

Mrigashira spans 23°20′ Vrishabha to 6°40′ Mithuna. Its lord is Mars and its devata is Soma, the Moon god. Its symbol is the head of a deer, which stands for a curious, searching mind that seeks what is new.

Mrigashira is a Mridu, or soft, nakshatra. Tradition holds it good for gentle work: learning, music and the arts, making friends, short journeys, and wearing new clothes. It favours calm talks over hard decisions.

On a Thursday, the day of Guru, this searching mind is best given to study and to sincere questions put to a teacher.

Remedy: chant "Om Som Somaya Namah" 11 times and offer milk or water to a Shiva Linga.

Avoid starting new work in Rahu Kaal, 12:56 PM to 2:19 PM. Bhadra (Vishti karana) begins at 11:30 PM.
